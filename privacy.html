隐私政策

生效与更新日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

本政策说明 Duo 创意盒（DuoToys）应用、支持邮箱和本说明网站如何处理信息。开发者联系邮箱：alanxrhuang@gmail.com。

应用内的数据

应用无需注册账号。开发者不收集应用内的个人数据或使用活动，不设置追踪、广告或第三方分析 SDK，也没有接收玩法内容的开发者服务器。

玩法成绩、偏好、打字内容、镜次记录等保存在设备的应用存储中。兼容设备上的铰链读数仅用于即时交互，不发送给开发者。

音频、图片与分享

仅在你选择“导入音频”并通过系统“文件”选取文件后，应用才把选中的音频复制到应用存储，并在设备上读取标题、歌手和内嵌封面图片。应用不会自动扫描你未选择的文件，也不会把这些内容上传给开发者。当前玩法无需访问整个照片图库，不使用相机或麦克风录制。

打字机图片及拍拍板 CSV/PDF 等导出由你主动发起。只有你在系统分享菜单中选择接收方并完成操作后，相应内容才交给该目的地。若你选择存入照片、文件或其他应用，相关系统服务、文件提供方或接收方会按其政策处理这些内容；云端文件的下载、设备备份或同步也由你使用的系统服务管理。

支持邮件与网站

如果你主动发送支持邮件，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容及你附带的文件，用于回复和排查问题。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。可通过同一邮箱提出查阅或删除支持邮件信息的请求；删除请求不影响依法必须保留的信息。

本网站不使用分析脚本、广告、表单或追踪 Cookie。访问静态页面时，托管服务会收到提供网页所需的技术信息，例如 IP 地址、请求时间和浏览器信息，并可能保留服务日志；这些请求不包含应用内的玩法记录或导入内容。

保留、删除与更新

应用内提供的清除功能可删除相应记录。删除应用会移除其本机应用数据；你已导出到其他位置的文件及系统备份需在相应位置管理。重新从备份恢复可能恢复应用数据。应用没有需要注销的账号。

若数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面及日期。有隐私疑问，请联系 alanxrhuang@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: November 1, 2026

This policy covers the Duo 创意盒 (DuoToys) app, support email, and this informational website. Contact the developer at alanxrhuang@gmail.com.

Data in the app

No account is required. The developer does not collect personal data or usage activity from the app. The app has no tracking, advertising, third-party analytics SDK, or developer server that receives activity content.

Scores, preferences, typed content, shot logs, and similar records are stored in the app's storage on your device. On compatible devices, hinge readings are used for live interaction and are not sent to the developer.

Audio, images, and sharing

When you choose Import Audio and select files in the system Files picker, the app copies only those files into its storage and reads their title, artist, and embedded cover artwork locally. It does not automatically scan unselected files or upload the content to the developer. Current activities do not require access to your entire photo library and do not record from the camera or microphone.

You initiate exports, including typewriter images and clapperboard CSV/PDF files. Content is passed to a destination only when you select it and complete the system share action. If you choose Photos, Files, or another app, that service or recipient handles the content under its own policy. Your chosen system services also manage cloud-file downloads, device backups, and synchronization.

Support email and this website

If you email for help, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you send, for responding and troubleshooting. Please avoid unnecessary sensitive information. You can request access to or deletion of support correspondence through the same email, subject to any legally required retention.

This website uses no analytics scripts, ads, forms, or tracking cookies. The hosting service receives technical information needed to serve static pages, such as IP address, request time, and browser information, and may retain service logs. These page requests do not include app activity records or imported content.

Retention, deletion, and updates

Use available in-app clearing controls to remove the corresponding records. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Manage separately any exported files and system backups; restoring a backup may restore app data. There is no app account to close.

Changes to data handling will be reflected on this page with an updated date. For privacy questions, contact alanxrhuang@gmail.com.
