A small box of playful objects

DUO 创意盒 includes seven activities: an angle challenge, toast workshop, pocket accordion, ten tiny retro-style computers, cassette player, clapperboard, and paper typewriter.

Use touch controls on an ordinary iPhone. Physical folding is optional and only responds when compatible hardware and system input are available. Follow each activity's rotation prompt.

One purchase includes all seven activities, with no account, ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Core activities work offline. The app interface is primarily in Simplified Chinese, with some English labels.
