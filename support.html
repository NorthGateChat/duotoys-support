常见问题

没有折叠屏，可以玩吗？

可以。普通 iPhone 使用屏幕上的角度转盘、吐司盖、风箱、琴键、拍板和播放器按键。真实开合互动只在兼容硬件与系统提供铰链输入时启用。

怎么打开玩法、返回首页？

在首页左右滑动卡片，轻点进入。用玩法内的关闭或返回按钮回到货架。若出现旋转提示，请按提示调整设备方向。

风琴为什么没有声音？

先检查媒体音量及蓝牙/耳机输出。弹奏时需要给风箱送气：可拖动风箱，或在曲目面板中开启“自动风箱”。也可先播放曲谱试听。

吐司怎么合盖、开盖？

普通 iPhone 进入烤制后，向下拖动合盖、向上拖动开盖。真实铰链输入可用时，也可以通过设备开合控制。

怎么导入自己的音频？

打开磁带时光，轻点“导入音频”，在系统“文件”中选择可播放的音频。应用会复制所选文件，并读取其标题、歌手及内嵌封面。受保护或不受支持的文件可能无法播放；可先用内置的巴赫古典录音检查播放器。

内容会同步吗？怎么导出？

应用没有自建账号或云同步。记录和导入内容保存在本机。纸上打字机可通过系统分享导出图片，拍拍板可导出 CSV/PDF。备份与恢复由设备系统管理；请保留需要的导出文件。

需要订阅或另外购买玩法吗？

不需要。付费下载包含全部七种玩法，没有订阅、广告或 App 内购买。购买记录和退款事宜请通过你使用的 App Store 账户处理。

Quick answers

Do I need foldable hardware?

No. Use the onscreen dial, lid, bellows, keys, slate, and player buttons on an ordinary iPhone. Physical folding is used only when compatible device and system input are available.

How do I navigate?

Swipe between cards on the home shelf and tap to enter. Use the activity's close/back control to return. Follow any device rotation prompt. The interface is primarily in Simplified Chinese, with some English labels.

Why is the accordion silent?

Check media volume and your Bluetooth/headphone output. The instrument needs airflow: drag the bellows or enable 自动风箱 (Automatic bellows) in the song panel. You can also play a songbook preview.

How do I control the toaster?

On an ordinary iPhone, drag down to close the lid and drag up to open it. Compatible hinge input can control the lid when available.

How do I import audio?

In the cassette activity, tap 导入音频 (Import Audio) and select playable audio in Files. Selected files are copied locally; their title, artist, and embedded cover artwork are read on-device. Protected or unsupported files may not play. Try the bundled Bach recordings to check playback.

Where are records stored?

The app has no account or developer-operated cloud sync. Records and imported files are stored on your device. Share typewriter pages as images or clapperboard logs as CSV/PDF through the system share sheet. Device services manage backups and restoration; keep exports you want to retain.

Are there extra purchases?

The paid download includes all seven activities, with no subscriptions, ads, or in-app purchases. Manage purchase history and refund requests through your App Store account.
